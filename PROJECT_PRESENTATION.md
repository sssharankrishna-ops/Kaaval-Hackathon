
# KAAVAL AI: Advanced Forensic Reconstruction & Surveillance

## Project Detailed Content
KAAVAL is a cutting-edge AI-powered platform designed for law enforcement and forensic investigators. It specializes in two core domains:

1. **Forensic Facial Reconstruction**: Taking highly degraded, blurred, or aged images and using Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) to reconstruct clear, identifiable facial features. It also utilizes StyleGAN-based age progression to predict how individuals may appear years into the future.
2. **Intelligent Surveillance**: A high-speed video processing pipeline that can scan hours of CCTV footage in minutes to find specific persons of interest using deep facial embeddings and vector search (FAISS).

The system is designed with **Resilient AI Architecture**, meaning it can gracefully handle hardware failures by switching between GPU (CUDA) and CPU processing in real-time without crashing the main program.
